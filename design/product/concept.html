EquipmentManager — Concept

RimWorld modpublished (Steam Workshop)

Vision

EquipmentManager is a RimWorld mod that automatically equips every colonist with the right weapons, sidearms, and tools — driven by configurable rules rather than manual micromanagement. Players define loadouts once; the mod continuously assigns the best-matching ranged weapon, melee weapon, sidearms, and work tools to each pawn based on rules, stat weights, skills, passions, traits, and work assignments, keeping the colony combat- and labor-ready as gear, pawns, and the colony evolve.

Target users

  • RimWorld colony managers — want pawns kept armed and tooled without manually re-equipping after every raid, recruitment, or gear upgrade.
  • Mid-to-late-game / large-colony players — manage too many pawns for hand-managed equipment to be practical; need rule-based automation that scales.
  • Optimization-minded players — want fine-grained control over equipment selection via stat weights, stat limits, skill/passion weighting, and whitelists/blacklists rather than a one-size-fits-all auto-equip.
  • Heavily-modded-loadout players — run Simple Sidearms (sidearm loadouts) and Vanilla Factions Expanded, and need equipment automation that respects those mods' mechanics.

Value proposition

EquipmentManager removes the repetitive chore of manually arming and tooling colonists. Players configure reusable loadouts and weapon/tool rules once — assigning pawns to roles like Assault, Sniper, Support, Slasher, or Crusher — and the mod automatically picks the best available gear for each pawn from their rules, weighting by combat stats, skills, passions, and traits, and dropping or swapping gear as better items appear. Unlike vanilla's manual equip, it offers rule-based, stat-driven selection that respects sidearms (Simple Sidearms) and work-tool needs, so colonies stay optimally equipped with minimal player input.

Pillars

  • Rule-driven automationEquipment selection is governed by configurable, reusable rules (ranged, melee, tool, work-type), not manual per-pawn assignment.
  • Stat- and skill-aware selectionItems are scored by stat weights and stat limits, and pawns by skills, passions, and traits, so "best" is tunable per role.
  • Loadouts as rolesPawns are assigned named loadouts (primary weapon + sidearms + tools + gating conditions) that map to colony roles.
  • Mod-ecosystem awareFirst-class handling of Simple Sidearms (sidearm rules), with Vanilla Factions Expanded compatibility.
  • Continuous upkeepAssignments are re-evaluated and re-applied as gear, pawns, and rules change, with optional dropping of unassigned weapons.

Anti-Pillars

  • Not an apparel/medicine/food managerexplicitly NOT this — scope is weapons, sidearms, and work tools, not full outfit/policy management.
  • Not a reimplementation of shared utilitiesexplicitly NOT this — stat, filter, and helper logic is consumed from LordKuper.Common, not forked.
  • Not a manual equip UIexplicitly NOT this — the goal is automation, with manual control expressed as rules/whitelists/blacklists rather than hand-equipping.

Constraints

  • Platform: RimWorld mod; supported game versions 1.3–1.6.
  • Hard dependencies: Harmony, Simple Sidearms; on 1.6, LordKuper.Common.
  • Upstream contract: LordKuper.Common is an upstream integration contract, consumed (not editable from this repo).
  • Build / runtime: targets net48; references RimWorld assemblies, Harmony (compile-only), SimpleSidearms (compile-only); ships to 1.6/Assemblies/.
  • Published: live on Steam Workshop — changes affect existing subscribers and saved games.
